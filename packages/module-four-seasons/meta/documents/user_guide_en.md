# Four Seasons

Four Seasons adds a gentle, animated overlay to your shop — falling leaves, snowflakes, cherry blossoms, or sunflower petals drifting across every page. It's purely decorative: a quick way to give your shop a seasonal feel for autumn, winter, spring, or summer. Customers keep browsing and checking out exactly as before; nothing about how the shop works changes.

Once activated, the effect runs automatically in the background on every page. Leaves are shown by default, but you can switch to snow, blossoms, or sunflower petals at any time in the extension settings.
